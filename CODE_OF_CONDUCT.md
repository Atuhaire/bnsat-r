# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

BNSAT-R is committed to providing a respectful, constructive and technically rigorous environment for research and open-source collaboration.

We welcome participation from people of different backgrounds, experience levels and geographic locations.

Our objective is to encourage productive technical discussion and collaborative research.

---

## Expected Behaviour

Participants are expected to:

* Be respectful;
* Assume good faith;
* Focus criticism on ideas and technical work;
* Provide constructive feedback;
* Cite sources where appropriate;
* Respect intellectual contributions;
* Acknowledge the work of others;
* Encourage learning and knowledge sharing.

---

## Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviours are not acceptable:

* Harassment;
* Personal attacks;
* Threats;
* Discrimination;
* Intimidation;
* Deliberate disruption;
* Unauthorised security testing;
* Malicious activity;
* Publication of private information without consent.

---

## Technical Disagreement

Technical disagreement is welcome.

Contributors are encouraged to challenge:

* Research assumptions;
* Methodologies;
* Experimental designs;
* Results;
* Interpretations.

However, disagreement should remain professional and evidence-based.

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may take appropriate action when behaviour violates this Code of Conduct.

Possible actions include:

* Requesting clarification;
* Removing inappropriate content;
* Closing discussions;
* Temporarily restricting participation;
* Permanently restricting participation.
